# 15-minute wallet defense checklist

Do this today. Tick as you go.

## Structure
- [ ] I have a **vault** wallet (hardware or cold) that never connects to websites
- [ ] I have a separate **daily** wallet with a small balance
- [ ] I use a **burner** wallet for mints, airdrops and unfamiliar sites

## Seed phrases
- [ ] No seed phrase exists as a photo, screenshot, cloud note, email or chat message
- [ ] Seed phrases are stored offline, in at least two separate physical places
- [ ] I know no legitimate support will ever ask for my seed phrase

## Approvals
- [ ] I reviewed my token and NFT approvals with an approval checker (e.g. Revoke.cash) on every chain I use
- [ ] I revoked approvals I don't actively need, especially unlimited ones and `setApprovalForAll`
- [ ] I set a reminder to repeat this monthly

## Accounts
- [ ] Exchange accounts use an authenticator app or hardware key, not SMS
- [ ] Exchange withdrawals are restricted to an address whitelist
- [ ] My email account (the key to all others) has 2FA and a unique password
- [ ] X / Discord / Telegram have 2FA — compromised socials are used to phish your followers

## Habits
- [ ] I open dApps from bookmarks, not from links in DMs, replies or search ads
- [ ] I read every signature request; I reject anything I don't understand
- [ ] I keep a short note of my wallet addresses and the exchanges I use — needed fast in an incident
